                                                                                                                   Typical Applications

Coupled inductors are ideal for use in a variety of circuits including flyback, multi-output buck and SEPIC. They can also be used as common mode chokes. These
parts offer excellent coupling coefficient (k  0.98); and provide a wide range of inductance values, high efficiency and excellent current handling. In SEPIC
topologies, the required inductance for each winding is half the value needed for two separate inductors, allowing selection of a part with lower DCR and higher
current handling.
